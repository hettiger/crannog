# 0002. MIT license

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-30

## Context

crannog is open source and needs a license.

## Decision

crannog is licensed under the MIT license (`LICENSE`).
Source files do not carry per-file copyright headers.

## Alternatives

- Apache-2.0: permissive with patent clause.
- GPL-3.0: copyleft.
- BSD-3-Clause: permissive, similar to MIT.

## Consequences

Anyone may use, modify, and redistribute crannog, including commercially, as
long as the license notice is kept.
